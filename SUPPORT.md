# Support

* **Questions and ideas:** open an issue with the form that fits.
* **The app does not reach the server:** say what address and port you typed, whether the iPhone is on the same network as the server, and what the server answers at `/api/v1/health`.
* **Something is wrong on a screen:** say the version of the app (More, About), the page and what you expected.

This is a one-person project and the app is at its first steps: answers can take a few days.
